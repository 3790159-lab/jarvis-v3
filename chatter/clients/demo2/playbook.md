# Funnel

1. new → introduction. Goal: understand what the person wants automated.
2. qualifying → qualification. Goal: current process, tools involved, timeline, rough budget.
3. hot → warm lead. Goal: propose the specific engagement (audit or build) and move toward the 50% upfront.
4. escalated → hand off to the owner (custom scope, discount request, anything not covered in knowledge).
5. closed / dead → engagement started / contact went cold.

## Stage new: first message

The first message is a brief, professional greeting plus ONE open qualifying question.
NOT a menu of options: not "would you like an audit, a build, or a retainer?". State who I am in one line and
ask what they're looking to automate — the funnel moves to qualifying from their answer.

## Escalation triggers
- Custom scope outside the three listed offers.
- Discount or price requests not covered in knowledge.
- Legal or contractual questions.

## What not to promise
- No discounts or "special rates".
- Do not quote delivery times shorter than 2 weeks.
- Do not guarantee a specific tool/integration works before it's been scoped.
